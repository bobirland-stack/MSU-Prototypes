Accepting gifts of funding or equipment for your research

This guide covers donations from companies, foundations, and government agencies. In most cases, faculty complete one form and one email to the donor. The college’s Development Office coordinates the gift from start to finish, and the college research office (DER) manages the proposal record and routes it through export control and university approvals. Review the requirements below, then select the type of gift.

Important — these requirements apply to every gift

  • Do not sign documents provided by the donor. Agreements and transfer forms require an authorized university signatory; the Development Office will route each document to the appropriate signer.
  • Do not accept delivery, or authorize shipment, until the Development Office confirms the review is complete. The Proposal Processing Form (PPF) begins a review that includes export control; submitting the form is the first step, not the final clearance.
  • Do not provide or estimate a value for donated equipment. The valuation must come from the donor; university staff cannot assign it.

Select the type of gift

Procedures vary by gift type. Select the option that matches your situation. If the sponsor expects deliverables, reports, or intellectual property rights, the funding may be sponsored research rather than a gift — begin with “Gift or sponsored research?”

Equipment donation

Faculty steps

  1. Notify the Development Office.Contact the Development Office (see Contacts, below) as soon as a donation appears likely. Early notification prevents delays later in the process.
  2. Submit the Proposal Processing Form (PPF).Complete the Proposal Processing Form. Under Nature of Project, select “Research – Gift.” Include your research gift (RG) account number if you have one; if not, an account will be created during routing.
  3. Request written confirmation from the donor.Use the template below. An email reply from the donor serves as official documentation; a formal letter is not required.
  4. Forward the donor's response to the Development Office, copying the research office at der-tickets@egr.msu.edu.This completes the documentation required from you.
  5. Coordinate shipment and delivery with the Development Office.The Development Office will confirm when shipment may be authorized, prepare the gift-in-kind paperwork, and route all required signatures on arrival.

Managed for you

  • Proposal record and export control review with the college research office (DER)
  • Chair and Dean approval routing
  • Review and signature of any required agreement
  • The gift-in-kind (consignment) form
  • Donor credit, acknowledgment, and records
  • Account setup with Contracts and Grants

Template: documentation request to the donor

To complete Michigan State University's review, could you reply to this email confirming that [organization] is donating the equipment to Michigan State University as an unrestricted donation (title transfers on delivery, with nothing expected in return), and include:

- Quantity
- Model, part, and serial numbers
- Condition
- Your organization's estimated value and its basis, such as book value or fair market value (the valuation must come from the donor; MSU cannot assign it)
- Specification sheets, attached
- Export control status, if known
- A statement that you are authorized to make this donation on behalf of the organization

If these details already appear on a transfer or donation form you are preparing, attaching that form is sufficient.

Tax documentation: the university issues a gift acknowledgment letter, not a tax valuation or exemption document. Donors claiming a deduction for a non-cash gift valued over $5,000 must provide IRS Form 8283; the Development Office coordinates the required university signature.

Cash gift

Faculty steps

  1. Notify the Development Office.Contact the Development Office (see Contacts, below) as soon as a gift appears likely.
  2. Submit the Proposal Processing Form (PPF).Complete the Proposal Processing Form. Under Nature of Project, select “Research – Gift.” Include your research gift (RG) account number if you have one; if not, an account will be created during routing.

  4. Send any check to the college research office (DER); do not deposit it at the college.Research gift checks are deposited centrally to ensure your account is credited correctly.

Managed for you

  • Proposal record and routing with the college research office (DER)

  • Deposit and account crediting
  • Donor acknowledgment and records

Template: documentation request to the donor

To complete Michigan State University's review, could you reply to this email (or provide a brief letter) confirming that [organization] is providing a gift of $[amount] in support of [project or laboratory], as an unrestricted gift with no terms or restrictions applied and nothing expected in return, sent or signed by an individual authorized to commit the organization.

Equipment loan

Faculty steps

  1. Notify the Development Office that the equipment is a loan.Loans nearly always involve an agreement, which must be reviewed and signed by an authorized university official before shipment.
  2. Submit the Proposal Processing Form (PPF).Complete the Proposal Processing Form, noting in the description that the equipment is a loan.
  3. Forward the lender's agreement or terms to the Development Office, unsigned.The Development Office routes it for university review and authorized signature.
  4. Notify the Development Office of the shipping and delivery dates.Loaned equipment also completes export control review and is tracked by the university.

Managed for you

  • Agreement review and authorized signature
  • Proposal record and export control review
  • Loan documentation and tracking
  • All routing and records

Faculty are not authorized signatories for the university. Loan agreements — including loans to your own laboratory — must be signed by an authorized university official.

Federal agency donor

Faculty steps (complete sequence)

  1. Notify the Development Office.Contact the Development Office (see Contacts, below) as soon as a donation appears likely. Federal agencies — including military research organizations and national laboratories — transfer equipment using their own property forms, so early notification matters even more here.
  2. Submit the Proposal Processing Form (PPF).Complete the Proposal Processing Form. Under Nature of Project, select “Research – Gift.” Include your research gift (RG) account number if you have one; if not, an account will be created during routing.
  3. Request written confirmation from the agency.Use the template below. If the agency’s transfer form already lists the equipment details, attaching that form is sufficient.
  4. Forward the agency’s response to the Development Office, copying the research office at der-tickets@egr.msu.edu.This completes the documentation required from you.
  5. Do not sign the agency’s transfer form — forward it unsigned.Authority to accept government property on the university’s behalf is limited to specific signatories, even when the form is addressed to you. The Development Office routes it to the appropriate signer.
  6. Wait for the Development Office to confirm the processing track and shipment.Some federal transfers are processed as government property transfers rather than charitable gifts; in either case the equipment comes to your laboratory, and only the required paperwork differs. The Development Office confirms which track applies and when shipment may be authorized.
  7. Confirm delivery.Tell the Development Office when the equipment arrives; the gift-in-kind paperwork and signatures are handled from there.

Managed for you

  • Identifying the university’s authorized property signatory
  • Determining gift versus property-transfer processing
  • Proposal record and export control review with the college research office (DER)
  • Chair and Dean approval routing
  • The gift-in-kind paperwork on arrival
  • Donor credit, acknowledgment, and records

- Quantity

Gift or sponsored research?

Determining the correct track

Does the sponsor expect anything in return? Deliverables, technical reports, milestones, intellectual property rights, publication review, or specific outcomes?

  1. Yes: the funding is sponsored research, not a gift.It is submitted through the college research office (DER) as a proposal. Funding started on the gift track will be re-routed, which delays the award.
  2. No: unrestricted support is a gift.Select the matching gift type above and follow those steps.
  3. Uncertain: contact the Development Office before proceeding.A brief description of the offer is sufficient; the Development Office will confirm the correct track. Confirming early prevents delays.

Common examples

  • The sponsor asks for informal research updates — generally still a gift
  • Required technical reports or data — sponsored research
  • The sponsor requests intellectual property or first rights — sponsored research
  • Equipment accompanied by a testing agreement — contact the Development Office
  • Membership or consortium payments — contact the Development Office

The complete process, start to finish

Every stage of an equipment gift, including the steps managed on your behalf. Shown for transparency — the only stages requiring your action are marked "Faculty." Timelines vary with export control complexity and approval schedules, and federal property transfers may follow a modified track. Contact the Development Office at any point for the current status of your gift.

  1. FacultyNotification and Proposal Processing Form (PPF)You alert the Development Office and submit the PPF ("Research – Gift").
  2. Faculty + donorDonor documentationYou request written confirmation from the donor and forward it to the Development Office, copying the research office at der-tickets@egr.msu.edu.
  3. Research OfficeProposal record and export control reviewThe research office opens a proposal record, attaches the donor documentation, and initiates export control review.
  4. Development OfficeGift-in-kind form preparationThe Development Office drafts the gift-in-kind form and notifies the university’s central gift office that a gift is in process.
  5. Chair / DeanCollege approval routingThe research office routes the record electronically for Department Chair and Dean approval.
  6. Contracts & GrantsContracts and Grants reviewContracts and Grants Administration and the export control office complete their approvals; a research gift account is created here if needed.
  7. Development OfficeShipment authorizedThe Development Office confirms to you and the donor that shipment may proceed; any donor transfer documents are executed by the university's authorized signatory.
  8. FacultyDeliveryYou confirm arrival to the Development Office.
  9. Development / Chair / DeanGift-in-kind signaturesThe gift-in-kind form is signed by the college signatory, Department Chair, and Dean, then routed to the university’s central gift office and the college research office.
  10. Central Gift OfficeDonor credit and acknowledgmentThe gift is recorded, the donor receives credit and a university acknowledgment letter, and the equipment is added to university inventory.
