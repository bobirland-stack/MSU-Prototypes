The ECE program

What an ECE capstone looks like

ECE capstone (ECE 480) is a one-semester course in which a team of seniors takes an industry-sponsored problem from concept to a working, demonstrated system — built and validated in hardware, not just simulated. Recent teams have delivered custom PCBs, embedded firmware, RF and wireless links, robotic platforms, and instrumentation for companies and research labs.

Sponsorship is $5,000, which funds the team's project budget — boards, components, and prototyping — so the work isn't limited by what students can pay for themselves.
