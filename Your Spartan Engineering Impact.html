    Your privacy

    This page shows records the college already keeps about your giving: gifts, receipts, agreements, and what your gifts paid for.

    • Only you, and the people you have asked us to add, can open it.
    • The college learns something from this page only when you choose to send it, such as asking Casey to call.
    • Students appear by name only if they have agreed to it.
    • Other donors appear only as they have asked to be listed.
    • Your name is never listed publicly until you approve the wording.

    The page keeps security records, such as failed sign-in attempts, but nothing about what you look at or how you use it.

    How we'll contact you. We never send a link to sign in, and we never ask for your code, a password, bank details or a payment by email, text or phone. Every email from us about this page starts with the verification phrase you chose with Casey. Your code comes only in a letter from Casey Morrill or an email from the College of Engineering. If anything seems wrong, call 517-555-0142, the number on your gift agreement.

    Public records. Michigan State University is a public university, so some records about gifts, such as gift agreements, can be requested by anyone under Michigan's Freedom of Information Act. The university withholds what the law allows, such as personal financial information. If you'd like to give anonymously, or want to know what could be released, ask Casey or the gift planning office.

    If anything here is wrong or out of date, call or text Casey Morrill at 517-555-0142, and it will be corrected in the college's records.
