Notes for the marketing and web team (not part of the page)

Where it livesThe Alumni section currently holds four items: Board, Awards, Get Involved, and Update Your Information. Spartan Stories slots in as a fifth, at /alumni/spartan-stories, with the share form at /stories/share. No information-architecture changes needed anywhere else.
What gets reusedThe site runs Drupal 11 with the college's egrbs5 theme. The cards above follow the same pattern as the homepage's Latest News cards; the featured band follows the storybite pattern. The intake is one stock Webform. Nothing here needs custom development beyond a content type and a view.
A naming note for the directorThe university already runs a produced-content "Spartan Story Hub" at spartan.msu.edu. This program is the first-person complement, but the working title overlaps with it. The name is the alumni relations director's decision, and this collision belongs in that conversation.
Launch versus later, module by moduleThe launch page is the intro, the story grid, the three promises, and the share button. Everything else on this page is a later switch, shown here so the destination is visible: the filter chips are views on fields the form captures from day one; the quote and advice sections preview the collection pages, which are launch behavior once a question has three published public answers; the map band is the opt-in city layer; the numbers strip and the where-stories-lead card populate themselves as the corpus and the follow-up loop grow. Nothing needs new data capture, only volume.
Award-winner entries are College-told draftsThe twenty award-winner pages are drafted from the College's own award announcements and public professional sources, in the institutional voice, pending the program's normal review. Each ends with the standing invitation. Before any publish: status-check the pre-1965 graduates (Flinn, Wineman), confirm Conroy's title given the pending Abbott acquisition, and route Surbrook and Wieferich through departmental and official channels.
The award-winner entries are drafts from the public recordAll twenty College-told profiles come from the July 2026 awards research: College award pages, state biographies, and professional society records only. Invitations have not been sent. Review and invitations run in batches, the first five (Wirsing, Surbrook, Wieferich, Fleis, Tinsey) first per the research shortlist; each page keeps its address and becomes the person's first-person page if they respond. Nothing publishes before the program's normal review.
What the page no longer says out loudThe surface stopped explaining itself; these facts live here instead. Student pages keep their URL and convert to alumni pages at graduation, per the configuration. The sample corpus is sample. And the idea of linking a faculty page to the alumni stories that name them is logged as a V3 candidate in the configuration: the mention-loop email and page-to-page links are separate systems, and nothing on the page implies otherwise now.
Ask the record is a stand-in for the capstone's answers engineThis demo version is keyword retrieval running in the page, which makes it verbatim by construction. The capstone builds the semantic version under the bright lines in spartan-stories-answers-engine.md: quotes only, no answer without a source, consent-scoped indexing, no persona, and an automated verbatim checker. Unanswered queries feed the question bank.
Answers by question is a view, not a workloadQuestion IDs are captured at the source, so on the built site this index is a database view: every question's answers assemble themselves, zero reviewer effort, across all personas. In the spreadsheet era before the build, the reviewer hand-maintains only the collections past the three-answer floor; the full index arrives with the Drupal build or the capstone platform. The demo shows the built state.
External links are liveEvery chrome and footprint link on this page points at the real destination on engineering.msu.edu and adjacent MSU properties, verified July 2026, opening in a new tab. Only story cards, quotes, the map chips, and the share buttons stay inside this repo. The rule for any future edit: external links are real and verified or they don't ship; never a plausible-looking invented URL.
What the links doStory cards and the share buttons link into the working demo and intake in this repo, so the click-through behavior is real. On the live site those become the story pages and Webform at their real addresses.
