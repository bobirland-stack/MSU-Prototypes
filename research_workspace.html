You have the manuscript. Raise comments or a patent concern before the window closes, or it clears on its own.

Standard terms

illustrative · verify against current MSU policy

Defaults for industry-sponsored research agreements. Individual agreements may vary; each agreement’s detail lists its own terms.

Publication
A 30-day review window before submission, extendable up to 60 more days to file patents. Results are publishable after that, always.
Intellectual property
MSU keeps its background IP. Sponsored-project inventions follow a standard option-to-license path, negotiated by MSU Technologies.
Indirect costs
Charged at the federally negotiated rate.
Signature
Faculty do not sign. The authorized office commits MSU: Business Connect for industry-funded work, OSP for federal and non-profit.
Student IP
Students own the IP they create for coursework and may decline any NDA or assignment with no academic penalty.
Confidentiality
Information you mark confidential when you share it is handled under Michigan's CRIIA. Flag it at the time, not after.

Access & records

Who can see this

Named participants from each side, chosen by each side. MSU participants sign in with NetID; named ACME participants use a sponsored MSU affiliate login, requested by their MSU contact and reviewed annually, so access lapses by default. Working documents live here; MSU-side strategy does not.

Without an account

Turn updates are emailed in full to named participants. Key dates export as calendar files. Any component exports as a stamped, point-in-time snapshot for colleagues without accounts.

What's shared, and what's public record

Information marked confidential at the time it is shared is handled under Michigan’s CRIIA; flag it when you send it, not after.
