Point to the Center, don't duplicate it

The Center for Spartan Engineering owns the recruiting relationship and maintains a deep, current site for it. This section owns everything else a company does with the college, and the routing across all of it. Recruiting is one door here; behind it, we hand off to the Center rather than rebuild their pages. Today the "Careers" menu mixes three audiences; separating them removes the confusion.

This section owns
  • Research, capstones, giving, equipment, capabilities
  • The coordination across all of it (one conversation)
  • A thin recruiting entry that orients, then hands off
  • Records & paperwork (W-9, IP, brand)
The Center owns (we link out)
  • Career fairs, dates, registration
  • Post a job, Grow Your Brand
  • Employer Partners, Michigander Scholars
  • Co-op program, placement & data reports
Employee-facing (moves out of Careers)
  • College Employment (jobs at MSU)
  • Faculty/Staff Intranet
  • These belong with HR / staff resources, not a company menu

Notes for the web team: every "native page" is authorable by CFR staff in Drupal with existing components, no custom development. The recruiting page deliberately links out to the Center for Spartan Engineering rather than duplicating fair dates, job posting, or the co-op program, which the Center maintains. The placement, salary, and Michigan-employment figures shown in the numbers band now come from the Center's published reports (careers.egr.msu.edu/reports), which also resolves several illustrative figures from the concept; any remaining concept numbers (intern conversion, repeat-employer rate, per-department counts) still need real data before publication. Separating the company-facing recruiting entry into this section lets the "Careers" menu stop mixing students, companies, and employees.
